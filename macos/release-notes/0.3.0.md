**Zones to unlock, loot to carry, and a merchant to spend it at.**

- **Zone progression:** a saved profile. The starting zones are open, and winning a zone opens the next one in level order.
- **Your hero, your look:** the hero is a real character body, with race, sex, skin, face, hair and facial-hair dials, and wears its class's dungeon and tier sets as bosses drop the pieces.
- **Backpack and coins:** creatures drop copper, silver and gold, plus items in the five WoW qualities: potions, food, explosives and trinkets, rings, necklaces and cloaks. The bag is on B, the item bar on 1–4. Better gear is equipped as you pick it up.
- **Merchant:** coins go to a purse kept between runs, to spend on permanent upgrades: Might, Fortitude, Haste, Greed, starting potions, and more.
- **Level-up reroll and banish:** the merchant's Fortune and Banishment give a run fresh hands and let you strike unwanted spells from the pool (R and T, or the bumpers).
- **Rare elites:** every minute or two, a bigger, glowing named creature that drops a guaranteed blue item.
- **Loot you can see and hear:** WoW's own coin, item and potion sounds, and green, blue and purple beams over the sacks worth picking up.
- **Controllers:** gamepads work again, Bluetooth controllers included. The D-pad and stick move a focus across the cards, menus and zone picker, and A selects.
- **Settings:** Video, Sound and Controls tabs, with display mode, vertical sync, anti-aliasing, render scale, view distance, weather and more, all saved between launches.
