**Characters: make up to ten heroes, each with their own progress.**

- **Character select:** the game now opens on your characters, with each one's race, class and zones won. Pick one and its zones, armor and look come with it.
- **Create New Character:** race, gender and class, then the looks and a name. A random name is filled in for you; type your own or roll another. Names are 2 to 12 letters, as in the game.
- **Bound progress, shared purse:** each character unlocks zones and collects armor on its own. Gold and the merchant's upgrades belong to the account, so every character spends from the same purse and starts with the upgrades bought.
- **Delete Character:** with a confirmation. The purse and upgrades stay.
- **Your existing hero carries over:** the first launch turns your save into your first character, with every zone, piece of armor and coin, under a newly drawn name.

Already on 0.3.x? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
