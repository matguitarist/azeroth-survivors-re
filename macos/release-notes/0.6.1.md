**Sharper and faster on big Retina screens, with Apple's MetalFX.**

- **MetalFX upscaling:** when the 3D world renders below your screen's resolution, Apple's MetalFX now scales it back up instead of a plain stretch: textures and edges stay crisp, and the interface is always drawn at full resolution. In Survivors and Classic alike.
- **Automatic resolution on large screens:** with Render scale at 100%, the game keeps the 3D near 2560×1600 pixels and lets MetalFX fill the rest. On a 5K display that is half the pixels to draw: a crowded Survivors fight needs about a quarter of the graphics work, and Classic in Shadowglen went from about 21 to a steady 60 fps on an M4. MacBook screens stay native; pick any other Render scale in Settings › Graphics to choose your own.
- **Home server:** the server set up on a home server now starts again by itself after the machine restarts (Unraid), and stays stopped if you stopped it from the launcher.

Already on 0.6.0? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
