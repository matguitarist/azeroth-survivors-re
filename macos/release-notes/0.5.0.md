**Classic: the original game, on your own Mac.**

- **Survivors or Classic:** the launcher now plays either mode. Classic is the original World of Warcraft 1.12.1.
- **A server on this Mac:** one click sets up a vmangos server in Docker (OrbStack or Docker Desktop): about 1 GB to download and a few minutes, once. It starts when the launcher opens and stops when you quit. Your characters stay on your Mac.
- **Accounts from the launcher:** create accounts, change passwords and give administrator rights in Settings › Server. A full map extraction (line of sight and pathing) can run in the background from there too.
- **Another server:** or enter the address of a 1.12.1 server that doesn't require the Warden anticheat.
- **AzerothPlus addon:** a cooldown manager, a damage meter, a swing timer and a personal resource display. Type /ap in the game.
- **AzerothAdmin addon:** game master tools for your own server, from the minimap button or /aa: levels, gold, cheats, teleports, items, and party bots to fill a group for dungeons.

Already on 0.4.1? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
