**Widescreen loading screens, drawn from each zone itself.**

- **A loading screen for every zone:** the first time a zone stands behind the menu, the game takes its picture at your screen's own shape and resolution. From then on that zone loads behind it, edge to edge, under its name.
- **No more black bars:** until a zone has its picture, the classic art fills a wide screen with a dimmed copy of itself instead of black bars.
- **Straight into your zone:** the game now loads your character's zone at launch, instead of loading the Barrens first and then moving.

Already on 0.4.0? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
