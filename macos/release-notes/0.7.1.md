**More life on your home server: bots that come to you, and the world's rates in your hands.**

- **Bots come to where you play:** a new option in Settings › Home Server › Bots sends bots of a fitting level to the zones around anyone playing, and has them move on every half hour to two hours instead of up to two days, so the zone you level in fills up during a session.
- **Keep bots near your level:** an optional limit of five levels above the highest player online, for a server where everyone levels together. Bots above it are made again at a lower level.
- **Rates:** experience, loot, gold, reputation and rested experience, from ×1 (the original game) to ×5. Quest items drop as before.
- One Apply for every server setting, with Revert for changes you haven't applied.
- **Faster database:** the home server's database keeps every table in memory (1 GB), for quicker starts.

Already on 0.7.0? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
