**A world map, a livelier pick screen, and one-click replays.**

- **World map on M:** the zone's real WoW map, with where you've been, the arena's edge, loot, creatures, bosses and your hero's arrow. LB on a controller.
- **The pick screen shows the zone you pick:** the scene behind the menu moves to the selected zone, without the full loading screen. The menu stays usable while the zone loads.
- **Play Again, Main Menu, Exit:** after a run, Play Again starts a fresh one right away with the same hero and zone. Enter plays again, Escape (or B) goes to the main menu.
- **Smoother late runs:** fewer leftover corpses and XP gems on the ground, so long fights stay at a steady frame rate.
- **Launcher settings match the game's:** the same video rows and steps as the in-game Settings, including Maximized display, Spell Detail, Full-Screen Glow and the four weather levels. The old weather switch did nothing and is gone.

Already on 0.3.0? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
