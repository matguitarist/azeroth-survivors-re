**Classic with company: a server full of bots, at home or on your Mac.**

- **CMaNGOS with bots:** a second server to pick in Classic, with a hundred bots questing, grouping and trading in the world. The launcher builds it on your Mac the first time (about 25 minutes). It keeps its own accounts and characters, separate from vmangos.
- **On a home server:** or set the same server up on an always-on machine on your network, an Unraid box for example, and manage it from the launcher over SSH: setup, start and stop, accounts, the bot count. It stays on your network; nothing is opened to the internet.
- **AzerothPlus, newer-client comforts:** every feature is a box in /ap, or in the new minimap button.
  - Smaller player and target frames near the center of the screen.
  - All Objectives: every quest in your log with its objectives, down the right of the screen.
  - Map & quest log: a smaller world map, resizable, with the zone's quests beside it, a search box and the zone's level range.
  - Your coordinates under the minimap, and the buff icons moved away from it. "Move Frames" drags them anywhere.
- **Fixes:** the cooldown manager no longer shows held or days-long cooldowns (the "597h" over the player frame).

Already on 0.5.0? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
