**A roomier Settings window, a Logs window, a fuller menu bar, and Help.**

- **Settings** now has a sidebar like System Settings, can be resized, and scrolls. The home server has three panes: Home Server, Bots & Rates, and Backups & Updates. A green, orange or red dot shows each server's state at a glance.
- **Logs window** (Window › Logs, ⌘L): the game's log and the servers' on this Mac and on your home server, with levels you can show or hide, search, the most frequent problems, and live following. The bots' harmless pathing and spell messages are set apart, so errors are the real ones.
- **Menu bar:** a Server menu to start, stop, restart and back up your servers; the mode and Classic server in the Game menu; shortcuts to each settings pane.
- **Help** (⌘?): getting started, Survivors and its controls, Classic and its addons, the home server, graphics, troubleshooting and keyboard shortcuts, searchable. Release notes and problem reports are a click away in the Help menu.

Already on 0.7.1? The app offers this update by itself, or use Azeroth Survivors → Check for Updates….
